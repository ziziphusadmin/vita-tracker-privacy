Common questions

My reminders aren't appearing

Reminders are scheduled on your device, so they depend on your system settings:

  • Check notifications are allowed for Vita Tracker in your device settings
  • Make sure reminders are switched on in the app, under Settings
  • On Android, battery optimisation can delay or suppress scheduled notifications — allowing the app to run in the background usually fixes it
  • If you changed your reminder times, open Settings and tap Refresh Reminders

How do I change when I'm reminded?

Settings → Reminder Times. Tap a slot to change it. With Vita Pro you can also set a custom time for each individual supplement.

I paid for Pro but don't see it

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. This re-checks your subscription with the App Store or Google Play and restores access — including after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. Use the same store account you bought with.

If it still doesn't appear, email us and mention roughly when you subscribed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from, not by us:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vita Tracker
  • Android: Google Play → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, since they are the seller. On iPhone use reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, request a refund through Google Play. If something went wrong on our side, email us and we'll help you sort it.

How do I export my history?

With Vita Pro, open History and tap Export Data (CSV). The file can be opened in any spreadsheet app.

Will I lose my data if I reinstall?

Yes. Your supplements and history are stored on your device, not on our servers, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them permanently and we cannot recover them. Export to CSV first if you want to keep a copy. Your subscription is separate and can always be restored.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. To also have your subscription record deleted from our servers, email support@ziziphus.io. See our Privacy Policy for what we hold and why.

Reporting a problem

If something looks broken, send us an email with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps enormously.
